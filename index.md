# TranscreveAI

TranscreveAI is an AI video analysis tool that turns public video URLs and media files into evidence-backed transcripts, OCR, summaries, and knowledge dossiers for agents and developers.

## What it does

- Probes a source before running a full analysis.
- Analyzes video and audio.
- Extracts transcripts, OCR, visual evidence, and structured JSON.
- Generates reusable Markdown knowledge files and evidence summaries.
- Indexes completed runs and answers follow-up questions from their evidence.
